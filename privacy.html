Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

8ctoMath is a maths game for children, so it is built to collect as little as possible: nothing leaves the device unless you deliberately switch on Sync. This page sets out exactly what is stored, where it stays, and how to remove it.

The short version

8ctoMath keeps each child's progress in a small file on your own device. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and it needs no account and no internet connection to play. If you never switch on Sync, nothing a child does in 8ctoMath ever leaves the device.

8ctoMath never asks you to allow anything: it uses none of the permissions Android asks about — no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. It uses the internet only for the optional Sync, for buying 8ctoMath+ through Google Play, and for opening this page from the parents' area.

What is stored on the device

For each child you add, 8ctoMath stores one small file in the app's own private storage:

  • the nickname typed in for that child, up to ten characters — a real first name is not needed, and a made-up one works just as well;
  • the day the child was added;
  • which kinds of calculations they practise and the level they have reached in each;
  • which numbers they tend to get wrong, as a set of weights, so those calculations come back more often;
  • their avatar, put together from the app's own pictures: a creature, a hat, a little extra, a backdrop and a colour;
  • their pearls, the stickers they own, where each sticker is stuck in the book, and the stickers they have given to a brother or sister on the same device;
  • the four-digit PIN a child may choose so that nobody else plays as them — optional, and a grown-up can take it off in the parents' area;
  • how many questions they answered and how many were wrong, per day, for the parents' weekly figures;
  • how long their last few quizzes took, which is what paces Brainy.

When a child is added, 8ctoMath also asks how old they are. The age only picks the kinds of calculations the first quiz asks and the level each one starts at, and is then forgotten: it is not stored.

Device settings — sound, music, voices, calm motion and language — are stored separately in the same private area, together with whether the rules of the game have been shown, whether 8ctoMath+ has been bought, which account Sync belongs to while you are signed in, and a random label the app makes up for itself when it is installed, so that pearls and stickers earned on two devices add up correctly.

Apart from the age above, we ask for nothing about a child, and we do not store a birthday, an age, an email address, a photograph, a contact, a location, or anything else about them. No identifier of your device or of your advertising profile is read or stored.

What is not in the app

  • No advertising.
  • Nothing a child can buy: the one purchase, 8ctoMath+, sits behind the grown-ups' gate.
  • No analytics or usage measurement, from us or anyone else.
  • No advertising identifier, no fingerprinting, no profiling.
  • No crash or performance reporting.
  • No social network, no chat, and no contact with anyone beyond the children on the same device.
  • No links out of the game, other than this page, an email link to us and Google Play's page for redeeming a code, all in the parents' area behind the grown-ups' gate.

8ctoMath+

8ctoMath+ is a one-time purchase, with no subscription, that opens every sticker, every sticker page and every part of the avatar, and includes Sync. It is bought through Google Play, behind the grown-ups' gate.

Google Play handles the payment under its own terms: we never see a name, an address or any payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether 8ctoMath+ has been bought and keeps that answer on the device. Codes for friends and family are redeemed on Google Play's own page.

Sync

Sync keeps a family's children the same on every device they play on, and keeps a copy of them in an account, so they survive a lost device. It is part of 8ctoMath+, it is off until you sign in and switch on Back up and sync in Parents → Sync, and the game is complete without it.

Signing in uses Firebase Authentication, with an email address and password or with your Google account; from a Google sign-in we receive the email address and display name of that account. Signing in on its own moves nothing. Once you switch Sync on, the per-child data listed above, including the random label of each device that earned pearls or stickers for a child, is copied to Google Cloud Firestore, hosted in Zürich, Switzerland, one document per child, and kept up to date from then on. It is sent over an encrypted connection and stored under your account. As with any Firebase-backed app, Google records the IP address and user agent of these requests for sign-in security.

Sync is used for nothing but keeping your devices in step and restoring them. It is never used to profile anyone, never sold, and never shared with a third party other than Google as the provider of the service above.

Cookies and tracking

8ctoMath uses no cookies, no advertising identifiers, no analytics and no tracking technologies. This website sets no cookies either. It is served by GitHub Pages, which — like any web server — records the IP address and user agent of each request in its own logs.

Children

8ctoMath is made for children, and is built to the standard that requires: no advertising, no analytics, no third-party tracking, nothing a child can buy, and no personal data collected about a child beyond the nickname typed in when they are added and the PIN they may choose. Everything that could matter to a parent — settings, statistics, levels, deleting a child, 8ctoMath+ and Sync — sits behind a gate that asks for a number written out in words to be typed in digits.

Because no contact details of a child are ever collected, we cannot and do not contact a child.

Data retention

Data on the device is kept for as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling 8ctoMath removes the app's private storage, and with it every child's progress on that device. If you use Sync, deleting a child removes them from the account as well, and from every device signed in to it.

Data sharing

We do not share personal data with anyone. If you switch on Sync, the data described above is processed by Google as the operator of Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, on our behalf and for no other purpose. A purchase of 8ctoMath+ is handled by Google Play under Google's own terms.

Your rights

Since a child's data lives on your device, you control it directly: Parents → Children → Delete removes a child and everything belonging to them, and uninstalling the app removes all of it at once. If you have used Sync, deleting a child also removes them from the account, and Parents → Sync → Delete account removes the account and everything stored with it for good. The Data Deletion Policy sets this out step by step.

You may also write to us to ask what is stored about you, to receive a copy, or to have it deleted.

Changes to this policy

If we change what 8ctoMath collects, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
